When lying on their tummy, your baby starts to push up and lift their head for a moment or two. It is the very first step in the long chain that ends in walking.

Short, frequent bursts of tummy time while they're awake help build the neck, shoulder and back muscles they'll need for everything else on this branch. If they protest, try it on your chest instead.
