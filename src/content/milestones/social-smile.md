Your baby smiles at you in response to your face or voice. It's the first real two-way social moment, and for many parents it's the point where the hard early weeks start to pay back.

Smiling back, talking and making eye contact all help build the bond. Babies born early often smile a bit later by the calendar, which is why this app can use corrected age.
