Your baby tries their first solid foods, usually at around 6 months. Signs they're ready: they can stay sitting up, they can coordinate eyes, hands and mouth, and they can swallow food rather than push it back out.

Early on it's about exploring tastes and textures. Milk still does most of the nutritional work.
